# New Joiner FAQ

**Q: When do I get paid?**
A: Salaries are paid on the last working day of each month. Payslips are in OrbitPeople > Payroll.

**Q: What is the core collaboration time?**
A: 11:00 to 16:00 IST. Meetings should be scheduled within this window. Wednesdays are "No Meeting Wednesdays" after 13:00.

**Q: Can I work remotely?**
A: Hybrid policy: at least 2 days per week in the office (Tuesday and Thursday are anchor days). Fully remote work abroad is allowed for up to 20 working days per year with manager approval.

**Q: How do I book a meeting room?**
A: Use the "OrbitSpaces" app or the Google Calendar room resources prefixed with "PUN-" (Pune) or "LIS-" (Lisbon).

**Q: Who do I ask about my probation?**
A: Probation is 3 months. Your manager runs a probation review in week 11 using the OrbitPeople "Probation Check-in" form.

**Q: Where do I find the org chart?**
A: OrbitPeople > Directory > Org Chart.
