# Leave and Benefits Policy (2026)

## Paid time off

- Full-time employees get 22 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year.
- PTO is pro-rated in the first year based on your joining date.
- Up to 5 unused PTO days can be carried over to the next year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March.
- PTO is requested in OrbitPeople under _Time Off > Request_. Your manager must approve at least 5 business days in advance for leave longer than 2 days.

## Recharge Fridays

- On the last Friday of every month the whole company is off. This is called "Recharge Friday".
- Recharge Fridays do not count against your PTO.

## Sick leave

- 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for more than 2 consecutive days.

## Parental leave

- Primary caregivers: 26 weeks paid. Secondary caregivers: 6 weeks paid.

## Contractors

- Contractors are not eligible for PTO, Recharge Fridays or the wellness stipend. Their leave is governed by their agency contract.

## Benefits

- Wellness stipend: INR 3,000 per month (or EUR 60 per month in Lisbon), claimable via OrbitPeople > Expenses.
- Learning budget: INR 50,000 per year for courses, books and conferences. Requests above INR 20,000 need manager approval.
- Health insurance covers the employee, spouse, two children and two parents.
