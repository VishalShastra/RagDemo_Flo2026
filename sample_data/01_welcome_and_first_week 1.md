# Welcome to Orbitly Systems - Your First Week

Orbitly Systems is a fictional B2B logistics-analytics company used for this RAG demo.
Headquarters: Pune, India. Secondary office: Lisbon, Portugal.

## Day 1 (Monday)

- Arrive at 09:30 at the Pune office reception on the 4th floor of "Skyline Tower B".
- Collect your laptop and your access badge from the IT Help Desk (room 4.12).
- Your onboarding buddy is assigned in the HR portal "OrbitPeople" under _My Team > Buddy_.
- Lunch on Day 1 is hosted by your manager at the cafeteria "Galaxy Cafe".

## Day 2 (Tuesday)

- Attend the mandatory "Orbit 101" orientation from 10:00 to 13:00 (room "Andromeda").
- Complete the security awareness course "SEC-AWARE-2026" in the learning portal OrbitLearn.

## Day 3 to Day 5

- Shadow your onboarding buddy for at least 6 hours.
- Set up your development environment following the IT Setup guide.
- On Friday at 16:00, attend the weekly "Launch Pad" all-hands meeting where new joiners introduce themselves in 60 seconds.

## 30-60-90 plan

- 30 days: ship your first pull request to production (the "First Orbit" milestone).
- 60 days: own one small feature end to end.
- 90 days: present a short demo in the Launch Pad all-hands.
